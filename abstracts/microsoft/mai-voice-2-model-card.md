# MAI-Voice-2 Model Card

[Official source](https://microsoft.ai/models/mai-voice-2/)

## Model summary

The next iteration in our speech synthesis family, MAI‑Voice‑2 is a prompted text‑to‑speech (TTS)

model that generates high‑fidelity, natural, and expressive speech across 15 languages. It captures

human-like intonation, rhythm, and emotional nuance, enabling engaging and lifelike conversational

experiences.
