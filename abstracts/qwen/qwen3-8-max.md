# Qwen3.8-Max

[Official source](https://help.aliyun.com/en/model-studio/qwen3-8-max)

A 2.4-trillion-parameter MoE flagship model with improved capabilities in coding and office scenarios, suitable for professional tasks such as law, finance, and design. It provides native visual understanding, supports semantic parsing of ultra-long documents and long videos, and supports autonomous planning and iteration for long-horizon tasks.
