## Description

Gemini Robotics On-Device is a state-of-the-art Vision-Language-Action (VLA) model based on our on-device Gemma models. It is designed for general-purpose robotic manipulation, operating eﬃciently on local devices. This model supports a wide range of tasks, scenes, and multiple robot types. It is currently available to a select group of trusted testers.
