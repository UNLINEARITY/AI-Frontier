# Gemini 3.7 Flash Model Card

[Official source](https://deepmind.google/models/model-cards/gemini-3-7-flash/)

## Description

Gemini 3.7 Flash is the next iteration in the Gemini 3 model family, featuring algorithmic improvements to its core reasoning foundation and support for agentic video understanding. It supports customizable thinking conﬁgurations to control the mix of quality, cost and latency.
