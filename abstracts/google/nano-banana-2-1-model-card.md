[Official source](https://deepmind.google/models/model-cards/nano-banana-2-1/)

## Description

Nano Banana 2.1 is a member of the Gemini 3 series of models, a suite of highly-capable, natively multimodal reasoning models. Nano Banana 2.1 can comprehend input from diﬀerent information sources, including text and images. Image and text output is generated in the response.
