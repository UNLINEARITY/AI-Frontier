# Introducing Muse Image and Muse Video

[Official source](https://ai.meta.com/blog/introducing-muse-image-muse-video-msl/)

We’re excited to launch Muse Image and preview Muse Video, the first media generation models developed by Meta Superintelligence Labs. Muse Image is our most advanced image generation model yet: it follows instructions faithfully, edits with precision, and composes from multiple references. It also brings agentic tool use capabilities and integrates with Muse Spark. Muse Video, built on the same pretraining base, delivers exceptional visual fidelity with native audio support.
