# SeedRealtime

[Official source](https://seed.bytedance.com/en/blog/seedrealtime-audio-visual-full-duplex-llm-released-toward-omni-modal-natural-interaction)

Today, we are officially launching SeedRealtime, a native audio-visual full-duplex LLM. As a key step toward omni-modal interaction, SeedRealtime uses a unified architecture to natively fuse audio, video, and text, enabling real-time interaction over continuous multimodal streams and delivering a brand-new "watch, listen, and speak" experience.
