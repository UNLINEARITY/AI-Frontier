# Seedance 2.5

[Official source](https://seed.bytedance.com/en/seedance2_5)

Seedance 2.5 is a next-generation audio-video joint generation model, built for 30-second storytelling with precise reference control and powerful editing capabilities.

Create videos up to 30 seconds in a single generation, with the option to extend twice for richer, more complete storytelling. Motion is smoother and more consistent, and the visuals are more realistic.

Understands reference videos more precisely -- capturing the intention, framing, and cinematic language to go beyond motion transfer into creative interpretation. Editing becomes more reliable, and responds to a wider range of audio and visual editing requests.

Provides advanced capabilities such as white-model control and green-screen editing, combined with professional camera movement and performance blocking, to better support complex video production and professional creative workflows.
