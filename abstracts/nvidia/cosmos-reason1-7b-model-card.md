# Cosmos Reason1

[Official source](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/Cosmos-Reason1-7B)

Cosmos Reason – an open, customizable, 7B-parameter reasoning vision language model (VLM) for physical AI and robotics - enables robots and vision AI agents to reason like humans, using prior knowledge, physics understanding and common sense to understand and act in the real world. This model understands space, time, and fundamental physics, and can serve as a planning model to reason what steps an embodied agent might take next.
